# Site news

Admins (with *Manage site settings and roles*) post short announcements
under **Admin → News**: a sentence or two in the same markup as the
wiki, optionally with a last day to show it.

The newest news that hasn't expired is shown at the top of every page,
with **Dismiss**. Dismissing hides it, and older news, for good: in a
cookie, and for logged-in users in their account too, so it stays hidden
on their other devices. Newer news shows again.

Editing news doesn't bring it back for those who dismissed it; post new
news for that. **Delete** takes news down at once, and **Restore** puts
it back. Posting, editing, deleting and restoring news go in the
[moderation log](moderation.md#the-moderation-log), and Danbooru apps
read the news at `/news_updates.json`.
