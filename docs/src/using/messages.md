# Messages

Users can send each other private messages (Danbooru's *dmails*).
**Messages** at the top of every page opens yours, with a count of those
you haven't read; **Send a message** on someone's profile, or **New
message**, writes one. Messages use the same formatting as comments.

Your **Inbox** has what you received, **Sent** what you sent, and
**Unread** what you haven't opened yet. Deleting a message removes your
copy only; the other person keeps theirs. **Mark unread** and **Mark all
read** do what they say.

## Blocking and reporting

**Block** (on a message, or **Block messages** on a profile) refuses that
user's messages from then on; the list of people you blocked is at the
bottom of your messages, each with **Unblock**.

**Report to the staff**, on a message you received, sends it to the
moderation queue (**Moderation → Reported messages**) with your reason.
Staff with *Hide comments and handle reports about them* see reported
messages there, and no other messages anyone sends; they mark each
**Settled** once dealt with, which goes in the moderation log.

## Limits

Sending needs the *Send private messages* permission (members, by
default; banned users can't), and each user can send a handful at once,
then about one a minute. Danbooru clients can read and send messages at
`/dmails.json`.
